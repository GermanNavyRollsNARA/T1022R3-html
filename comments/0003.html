This copy of an order is repeated for each U-boat throughout the roll.

The date of this document, 2 August 1914, shows that it was drafted when Britain had not yet joined the war (they declared war on 4 August, after Germany attacked Belgium). The French and Russians had mobilized before Germany, but Germany apparently declared war on them before vice versa. The German imperial government wanted Britain to stay neutral, and not harming British ships was a means to that end. The German government sent Belgium an ultimatum on 2 August, telling them to let the German Army pass through Belgium on the way to France.
