Top Secret.

Order for the security of the German Bight on 3 Aug [19]14. 1 ) Intelligence on the political situation: see secret order of 2 August of this year. Hostile actions against English warships and merchant vessels are to be absolutely avoided. Russian and French forces are to be treated as hostile. Nothing is known of the movements of enemy forces.

2 ) Security of the German Bight — for position see sketch. Primary task of the security patrol: protection of the German Bight against mining operations and submarines. The U-[boat ]line lies on a circular arc with a radius of 29 nautical miles from the Elbe One lightship.

3) The positions must be taken up by 4:30 a.m. on 3 August. Positions 1 and 2 are to be laid by S.100. Positions 3, 4, 5, 6, and 7 are to be taken up independently. Boats to be ready to dive and blacked out. Conduct in the event of enemy countermeasures during the advance to positions: see annex. If enemy forces are not in sight, after taking up the security line S.100 will inspect positions 1–7. S.100 then to U-boat harbor, Stettin-Hoschee buoy for protection of the U-boat harbor.

4) At positions, drop anchor buoys. As long as the outer torpedo boat line holds, the operational area is bounded by it. Should torpedo boats fall back before enemy forces, advance beyond the outer security line for attack is permitted.

5)Positions are to be vacated at 8 p.m. on 3 August or on S.K. orders. Boats to enter U-boat harbour.

6) Use of deck guns at the commander's discretion. The deck gun is to be kept manned as long as it is clear. Boats of the First U-Flotilla: deck gun manned if possible from 0h 0m to 0h 45m.

7) In fog: carry out the mission.
